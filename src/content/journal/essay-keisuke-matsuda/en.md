title: Thinking “Before the Result” in Keisuke Matsuda’s Paintings
summary: A consideration of the pre-intentional pictorial thinking found in Keisuke Matsuda’s practice.
hero_alt: Portrait of a man in a red jacket standing beside a wooden frame
---
Keisuke Matsuda was born in Gunma Prefecture in 1984 and currently lives in Kyoto Prefecture. In 2009, he completed the Painting program at the Graduate School of Arts, Kyoto City University of Arts. Matsuda, who says, “I try to make paintings that exist before the intention to arrive at a result,” explains that when the reality before him feels identical to the words in his mind, an image arises before he becomes consciously aware of it.

Rather than rendering that image as something retinal, he seeks to make paintings in which the image itself becomes bodily movement and intention.
